The `/ua-campaign` skill builds a full paid user acquisition plan across all major channels — Apple Search Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google UAC, and TikTok — with campaign structure, bidding strategy, creative specs, and a weekly optimization cadence calibrated to your budget and target CPI.

## Invoke

```
/ua-campaign
```

## What You Get

- **Budget-tiered channel allocation** — which channels to use based on your monthly spend (from under $1K to $100K+)
- **Campaign structure** — ad group hierarchy, keyword lists, audience targeting, and creative requirements per channel
- **Bidding strategy** — starting bids, CPA targets, and escalation rules for each campaign type
- **Creative specs** — video (15–30s), static, and carousel requirements with hook principles
- **Optimization cadence** — daily, weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly tasks to maintain performance

## Example Prompts

```
"Build an Apple Search Ads + Meta campaign plan for my fitness app with a $5K/month budget"
"My CPI is $4.50 but my LTV is only $6 — help me fix the unit economics"
"Create a creative brief for Meta video ads targeting health-conscious users"
```

## Channel Selection by Budget

| Monthly Budget | Recommended Channels |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Under $1K | Apple Search Ads Basic only |
| $1K–$5K | Apple Search Ads Advanced + 1 social channel |
| $5K–$20K | ASA + Meta + Google UAC |
| $20K–$100K | ASA + Meta + Google + TikTok |
| $100K+ | All channels + programmatic + influencer |

<Note>
  **Apple Search Ads has the highest intent of any paid channel** — users are already inside the App Store searching for something. Start here before any social channel. Even a $500/month ASA campaign will typically outperform a $2,000/month Meta campaign in install quality for most app categories.
</Note>

## Key Funnel Metrics

| Metric | Target (ASA) | Target (Social) |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Click-Through Rate | > 5% | > 1% |
| Install Conversion Rate | > 30% | > 10% |
| CPI | < LTV ÷ 3 | < LTV ÷ 3 |
| Day-7 ROAS | Predictive of long-term return | > 40% |

<Accordion title="What makes a high-performing video ad creative?">
  Hook in the first 3 seconds — show the app doing something remarkable, not a logo or title card. Keep it 15–30 seconds. Design for sound-off (most users see it muted) with captions or text overlays carrying the narrative. End with a clear CTA and App Store badge. Show the app in action — not lifestyle footage of someone "enjoying" the app.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I optimize after launch?">
  Daily: check spend pacing, pause overspending ad sets. Weekly: review CPI/CPA by keyword or ad set, adjust bids. Bi-weekly: refresh creative (ad fatigue typically sets in after 2–3 weeks). Monthly: review channel mix, reallocate budget to winning channels. Quarterly: strategic review and test one new channel.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Improving your **organic App Store conversion rate** directly lowers your effective CPI on every paid channel — more of the users you pay to send to your page will download. Run `/ab-test-store-listing` alongside your UA campaign for a compounding growth effect.
</Tip>
