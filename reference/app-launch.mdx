The `/app-launch` skill creates a detailed, date-stamped launch plan covering everything from ASO foundation and TestFlight beta to launch-day posting, press outreach, and first-month optimization — tailored to your budget, audience, and target countries.

## Invoke

```
/app-launch
```

## What You Get

- **8-week timeline** — phase-by-phase checklist with specific tasks and owners for each week
- **Channel strategy** — prioritized free channels (Product Hunt, Reddit, Hacker News, X) and paid channels (Apple Search Ads, Meta, TikTok) with launch-specific tactics
- **Press outreach templates** — personalized pitch email structure, press kit checklist, and embargo timing guidance
- **Post-launch monitoring plan** — daily tasks for week 1 and monthly optimization tasks for months 1–3

## Example Prompts

```
"Create an 8-week launch plan for my productivity app launching on March 15"
"I'm launching in 2 weeks with no budget — give me an organic-only launch checklist"
"Help me write a launch pitch for TechCrunch and Hacker News"
```

## Launch Timeline Overview

| Phase | Timing | Key Focus |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| ASO Foundation | Week –8 | Keywords, metadata, screenshots, icon |
| Pre-launch Page | Week –8 | Pre-order setup, beta recruitment |
| Beta & Content | Week –4 | TestFlight, social content, press kit |
| Final Prep | Week –1 | App review submission, ASA campaign setup |
| Launch Day | Day 0 | Press release, social posts, Product Hunt |
| Week 1 | Days 1–7 | Review responses, keyword monitoring, ASA optimization |
| Month 1 | Weeks 2–4 | Full ASO audit, screenshot A/B test, update planning |

<Note>
  Submit your app for App Review **at least 2–3 days before** your intended launch date. Apple's review process typically takes 24–48 hours but can take longer. Missing your launch window by a day can cost you coordinated press coverage and community momentum that's hard to rebuild.
</Note>

<Accordion title="What free launch channels work best for indie developers?">
  **Product Hunt** works well for productivity, developer tools, and design apps. **Hacker News (Show HN)** is best for technical or innovative apps with a developer story. **Reddit** requires genuine community participation — find 3–5 relevant subreddits and contribute before you promote. **X/Twitter** build-in-public threads consistently outperform pure launch announcements because they bring the audience along for the journey.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What should I do on launch day itself?">
  Morning: release the app (or confirm phased release started), send press emails with embargo lift, post on all social channels, post on Product Hunt if applicable. Throughout the day: monitor App Store Connect, respond to every review, engage social mentions, watch crash reports. Evening: thank early adopters, share day-one milestones publicly, plan tomorrow's content.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Brief your **TestFlight beta users** to be ready to review on launch day. Real early reviews with genuine sentiment carry significantly more weight than a spike of reviews that appear overnight. Aim for 10–20 authentic 5-star reviews in the first 48 hours.
</Tip>
