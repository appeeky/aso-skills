The `/screenshot-optimization` skill builds a complete screenshot strategy — from hook to call-to-action — covering all 10 App Store slots with design briefs, text overlay copy, layout guidance, and a side-by-side competitor audit.

## Invoke

```
/screenshot-optimization
```

## What You Get

- **10-slot screenshot plan** — per-slot brief with caption copy, screen to show, layout type, and visual focal point
- **Text overlay copy** — benefit-driven headlines (4–6 words each) for every slot
- **Design brief** — color palette, font recommendations, layout templates, and device frame guidance for your designer (or for DIY tools)
- **Competitor screenshot audit** — structured comparison of your creative against up to 3 competitors

## Example Prompts

```
"Create a screenshot strategy for my meditation app (id: 6447810428)"
"My first screenshot isn't converting — give me 3 better hook options"
"Audit my screenshots against Headspace and Calm"
```

## MCP Tools Used

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `get_app` | Fetch your current screenshots, metadata, and rating |
| `get_competitor_screenshots` | Pull competitor screenshot sets for the audit table |
| `get_category_screenshots` | Browse screenshot creative from top apps in your category |

## Screenshot Slot Framework

| Slot | Role | Goal |
|------|------|------|
| 1 | **Hook** | Answer "What does this do and why do I need it?" |
| 2–3 | **Core value** | Show the two most compelling features |
| 4–7 | **Feature showcase** | One benefit per slot with supporting UI |
| 8–9 | **Trust & differentiation** | Social proof, awards, unique advantages |
| 10 | **Call to action** | Recap + download trigger |

<Note>
  Users decide whether to download within **3–6 seconds** of landing on your product page, and the first three screenshots (visible without scrolling) drive 80% of that decision. Use benefit-driven captions in your text overlays — they communicate value instantly even before a user reads your description.
</Note>

<Accordion title="What makes a high-converting first screenshot?">
  The first screenshot must answer "What does this app do and why should I care?" in under 3 seconds. Proven patterns include: a benefit headline paired with your core UI screen, a before/after transformation, or social proof ("5M+ users") anchored to your main feature. Avoid welcome screens, login screens, or any screen that doesn't demonstrate immediate value.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I use device frames or go frameless?">
  Both work — the key is consistency. Frameless (full-bleed UI) feels modern and shows more content. Device frames add context for utility apps where the "app on a phone" framing is part of the story. Test both using `/ab-test-store-listing` if your volume supports it.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Always test your screenshots at thumbnail size (roughly 120×214px for portrait) — that's how they appear in search results. If your text overlay is unreadable at that size, increase your font size before publishing.
</Tip>
