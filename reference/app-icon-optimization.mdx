The `/app-icon-optimization` skill audits your current icon, analyzes how your category's top apps look, identifies differentiation opportunities, and produces a designer-ready brief — plus a step-by-step A/B testing plan using Apple Product Page Optimization or Google Play Experiments.

## Invoke

```
/app-icon-optimization
```

## What You Get

- **Icon audit score** — rated across clarity at 60×60px, color contrast, category differentiation, simplicity, and brand alignment
- **Category differentiation analysis** — visual map of how your top 20 competitors look so you can stand out
- **Designer brief** — brand colors, mood, reference icons, required deliverables, and test-size mockup requirements
- **A/B testing setup guide** — step-by-step for Apple PPO (up to 3 variants) and Google Play Experiments

## Example Prompts

```
"Audit my app icon and compare it to the top 10 productivity apps"
"Brief a designer to create 3 icon variants for an A/B test"
"My icon looks identical to my competitors — help me differentiate"
```

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `get_app` | Fetch your current icon and metadata for the audit |

## Icon Design Principles

| Principle | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Simplicity** | Maximum 2 elements — detail disappears at 60×60px |
| **Color contrast** | Works on both white (light mode) and dark backgrounds |
| **Recognizable mark** | Describable in 3 words or fewer |
| **No text** | Illegible at small sizes; Apple discourages it |
| **Category differentiation** | Visually distinct from your top 20 competitors |

<Note>
  Your icon appears in **every App Store search result** — it's the very first conversion point, before users read your title, see your rating, or view a single screenshot. A strong icon can lift tap-through rate by 20–40% with no other changes to your listing.
</Note>

<Accordion title="How do I set up an icon A/B test on iOS?">
  Go to **App Store Connect → Your App → Product Page Optimization → Create Test**. Upload up to 3 icon variants, set traffic allocation (20–33% per variant), and run for a minimum of 7 days. The primary metric is install conversion rate (impressions → installs). You need at least 1,000 impressions per variant for a reliable signal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's the best thing to test first?">
  Test one variable at a time: color scheme (same mark, three background colors), mark style (flat vs illustrated vs 3D), or dark vs light background. Avoid testing subtle variations — the difference needs to be significant enough to detect statistically. Start with the change you hypothesize will have the biggest visual impact on your category page.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Before finalizing any icon design, export it at **60×60px** and view it in a mocked-up App Store search results row alongside your top competitors. This is the exact context users see — and it's the only quality gate that matters.
</Tip>
