The App Marketing Context skill is the foundation of the entire ASO Skills library. It walks you through a structured interview about your app — its category, value proposition, target audience, competitors, and goals — and saves everything to a file called `app-marketing-context.md` in your project root. Every other skill checks for this file first and uses it automatically, so you never have to repeat yourself.

Think of it as a one-time setup that makes every subsequent skill smarter and faster.

## Invoke

```
/app-marketing-context
```

You can also describe your goal naturally and let the router handle it:

```
/aso-skill  Set up my app context before I run any other skills
/aso-skill  Create a marketing context file for my fitness app
/aso-skill  Update my app-marketing-context — I just launched on Android
```

<Tip>
  **Run this skill first, before any other skill.** Every other skill in the library opens by checking for `app-marketing-context.md`. When it exists, skills skip redundant questions and deliver sharper, more relevant output. Five minutes here saves time on every skill you run after.
</Tip>

## Inputs

The skill walks you through each section interactively. Here's what it will ask:

<ParamField path="app_id_ios" type="string">
  Your Apple numeric App ID (e.g. `1617391485`). Used to auto-populate your current metadata, rating, and keyword rankings when the Appeeky MCP is connected.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="app_id_android" type="string">
  Your Google Play package name (e.g. `com.example.myapp`), if your app is on Android.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="app_description" type="string" required>
  A plain-language description of what your app does and the problem it solves. Don't worry about marketing language — explain it the way you'd describe it to a friend.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="target_audience" type="string" required>
  Who your ideal user is — their demographics, behaviors, and pain points. The more specific, the better. Example: "Women 25–40 who want to build consistent morning routines but struggle with motivation."
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="main_competitors" type="array">
  Your top 3–5 competitors by name or App ID. If you're unsure, the skill can suggest competitors based on your category and seed keywords.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="goals" type="array" required>
  Your top 1–3 goals with target metrics and timelines. Example: `["Reach top 20 in Health & Fitness by March", "Grow to 10,000 monthly active users by Q2", "Hit $5,000 MRR from subscriptions by June"]`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="budget_and_team" type="string">
  Your approximate monthly marketing budget and team size (solo, small team, or full marketing team). Helps skills calibrate recommendations to what's actually executable for you.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="markets" type="array" default='["US"]'>
  The countries or regions you're targeting, in priority order. Used by skills like `/keyword-research` and `/localization` to scope their output.
</ParamField>

## Output

### Saved Context File

The skill creates `app-marketing-context.md` in your project root with the following structure:

```markdown
## App Overview
- **App Name:** Habitly
- **App ID (Apple):** 1617391485
- **App ID (Google Play):** com.example.habitly
- **Category:** Health & Fitness
- **Platform:** iOS + Android
- **Price Model:** Freemium (subscription)
- **Launch Date:** March 2024

## Value Proposition
- **Problem:** People want to build lasting habits but quit after a few days
- **Target Audience:** Adults 25–40 building productivity and wellness routines
- **Unique Differentiator:** Streak-based motivation with social accountability
- **Elevator Pitch:** Habitly turns habit building into a daily game you actually want to play

## Competitors
| App | App ID | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----|--------|-----------|------------|
| Streaks | 963034692 | Clean UX, iOS integration | No Android, no social features |
| Habitica | 994882113 | Gamification, community | Overwhelming for new users |
| Done | 935220885 | Simplicity | Limited analytics |

## Current ASO State
- **Title:** Habitly — Daily Habit Tracker
- **Rating:** 4.7 (2,341 ratings)
- **Primary Keywords:** habit tracker, daily routine, streak

## Goals
1. Reach top 20 in Health & Fitness by March — Target: chart rank ≤ 20
2. Grow to 10,000 MAU by Q2 — Target: 10,000 MAU
3. Hit $5,000 MRR from subscriptions by June — Target: $5,000 MRR

## Resources
- **Budget:** $500/month
- **Team:** Solo developer
- **Markets:** US (primary), UK, Canada (secondary)
```

### Recommended Next Steps

After saving the file, the skill summarizes your key strengths to leverage, obvious gaps to address, and the two or three skills you should run next — typically `/aso-audit` to establish a baseline score, then `/keyword-research` to build your keyword strategy.

## MCP Tools Used

| Tool | What It Fetches |
|---|---|
| `get_app` | Current metadata, ratings, category, and platform details for your App ID |
| `get_app_keywords` | Keywords your app currently ranks for — pre-populates the ASO State section |
| `search_apps` | Helps identify competitors if you don't have their App IDs handy |

## Tips

<Tip>
  **Update your context when anything significant changes.** Got a new competitor? Changed your target audience? Launched in a new country? Re-run `/app-marketing-context` to update the file. All subsequent skills will automatically use the latest information.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Be specific with your goals.** "Grow my app" is not a goal the skills can optimize for. "Reach top 10 in Productivity by Q2" is. The more specific your goals, the more directly every other skill can align its recommendations to what actually matters to you.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **List competitors you aspire to beat, not just your direct peers.** Including one or two larger, more successful apps in your context file means skills like `/competitor-analysis` and `/keyword-research` will surface the tactics and keywords those market leaders use — giving you a roadmap for where you want to be, not just where you are today.
</Tip>
