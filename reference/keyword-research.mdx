The Keyword Research skill helps you find, evaluate, and prioritize the keywords your app should target in the App Store or Google Play. It expands from your seed keywords using competitor data, search suggestions, and category analysis, then scores every candidate on a combined Opportunity Score — so you always know which keywords to go after first and which to save for later.

## Invoke

```
/keyword-research
```

You can also describe your goal naturally and let the router handle it:

```
/aso-skill  Find the best keywords for a meditation app
/aso-skill  What keywords should I target for a fitness tracker in the UK?
/aso-skill  Show me keywords my competitors rank for that I don't
```

<Note>
  If you have an existing app, provide your App ID so the skill can pull your current keyword rankings. This makes the gap analysis significantly more useful — you'll see exactly where you already have ranking momentum versus where you're starting from zero.
</Note>

## Inputs

<ParamField path="app_id" type="string">
  Your Apple numeric App ID or Google Play package name. Optional, but strongly recommended — it lets the skill compare your current rankings against competitors and identify gaps.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="target_country" type="string" default="US">
  The country market you want keywords for. Search volume, difficulty, and competition vary significantly by country.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="seed_keywords" type="array" required>
  Three to five keywords that describe your app's core function. These are your starting point — the skill expands each one into dozens of candidates. Example: `["habit tracker", "daily routine", "productivity"]`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="intent" type="string" default="downloads">
  Your primary optimization goal: `downloads` (maximize installs), `revenue` (target high-value users likely to subscribe or purchase), or `brand` (rank for your app or company name).
</ParamField>

## Research Process

The skill runs four phases to build your keyword strategy:

<Accordion title="Phase 1 — Seed Expansion">
  Your seed keywords are expanded using App Store autocomplete suggestions, competitor keyword analysis, category-level research, synonyms, related terms, and common misspellings. Long-tail suggestions are flagged separately — they often carry lower competition than head terms.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Phase 2 — Keyword Evaluation">
  Each candidate is evaluated on four signals: **Search Volume** (estimated monthly search demand, 1–100 scale), **Difficulty** (competition for the keyword, 1–100 scale), **Relevance** (how closely the keyword matches your app's function), and **Intent** (whether the searcher is likely to download, not just browse).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Phase 3 — Opportunity Scoring">
  Every keyword receives an Opportunity Score calculated as:

  ```
  Opportunity Score = Volume × (1 − Difficulty) × Relevance  [normalized 0–100]
  ```

  All three inputs are on a 0–1 scale before multiplication: Volume and Difficulty are normalized from their raw 1–100 scores, and Relevance is your manual 0–1 assessment. This multiplicative formula means a keyword weak on any single dimension — very high difficulty, very low volume, or poor relevance — scores low overall, regardless of how strong the other two signals are.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Phase 4 — Keyword Grouping">
  Keywords are sorted into three strategic tiers: **Primary** (top 5–7 keywords by Opportunity Score — place in your title and subtitle), **Secondary** (keywords ranked 8–20 — target in subtitle and keyword field), and **Long-tail** (all remaining relevant keywords — fill the rest of your keyword field and rotate based on performance).
</Accordion>

## Tips

<Tip>
  **Never repeat keywords across fields.** Apple indexes the title, subtitle, and keyword field independently. Putting the same word in all three wastes your character budget without improving rankings — use each field to cover different keywords.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Use singular forms in the keyword field.** Apple automatically indexes both singular and plural forms, so `tracker` covers both "tracker" and "trackers". This saves you characters for additional unique keywords.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Refresh your keyword strategy every quarter.** Search trends shift with seasons, cultural moments, and competitor moves. Set a reminder to re-run this skill every 90 days and compare Opportunity Scores against your current rankings.
</Tip>
