The `/monetization-strategy` skill analyzes your current monetization model, recommends pricing tiers, paywall timing, and trial length, and benchmarks your conversion rate and ARPU against category norms — giving you a prioritized roadmap to increase revenue without hurting user satisfaction.

## Invoke

```
/monetization-strategy
```

## What You Get

- **Pricing tier analysis** — recommended monthly, annual, and optional lifetime prices based on category benchmarks and competitor data
- **Paywall timing recommendations** — when to gate (after aha moment, feature-based, time-based trial) and which converts best for your app type
- **Trial optimization plan** — trial length, in-trial message sequence (Day 1, 3, 5), countdown nudge, and end-of-trial offer strategy
- **Category benchmarks** — conversion rate, ARPU, and trial-to-paid targets for your specific category

## Example Prompts

```
"Analyze my monetization strategy for a productivity app with a $4.99/month subscription"
"My trial-to-paid rate is 22% — what's a realistic target and how do I improve it?"
"Should I offer a lifetime purchase option alongside my subscription?"
```

| Photo & Video | $4.99–$9.99 | $29.99–$49.99 | 40–60% |

## Paywall Timing Options

| Timing | Conversion Rate | Best For |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| Onboarding (before value) | 2–5% | Strong brands only |
| After aha moment | 5–10% | Most apps |
| Feature gate (when they need premium) | 8–15% | Utility and productivity |
| Usage limit (after N uses) | 5–8% | Content and tools |
| Time-based trial | 5–10% | Complex apps |

<Note>
  Anchoring your **annual plan** as the "Best Value" option while displaying the monthly plan as the default reference price is one of the highest-leverage pricing tactics available. Show the weekly equivalent cost for expensive subscriptions ("Just $1.92/week") to reduce sticker shock.
</Note>

<Accordion title="Should I offer a lifetime purchase option?">
  Lifetime purchases work well as a **one-time cash flow boost** and appeal to users who are philosophically opposed to subscriptions. Price it at 2–3× the annual rate. Be aware it creates a cohort of users who stop paying — track LTV carefully and limit or remove the lifetime option once your subscription base reaches critical mass.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long should my free trial be?">
  7 days works for most apps — long enough to form a habit, short enough to force a decision. Use 3-day trials for simple utility apps where value is immediate, and 14-day trials for complex tools where users need more time. Send value-reminder notifications on Days 1, 3, and 5 of the trial, and a countdown nudge on the last day with a discounted first-month offer.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Test raising your price before optimizing your paywall design. Many apps are significantly underpriced relative to the value they deliver — a 30% price increase with a 5% drop in conversion rate is still a net revenue win.
</Tip>
