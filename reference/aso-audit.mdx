The ASO Audit skill performs a comprehensive health check of your App Store or Google Play listing. It scores your app across 10 weighted factors on a 0–100 scale, identifies what's dragging your rankings down, and delivers a prioritized action plan — split into Quick Wins you can ship today, High-Impact Changes for this week, and Strategic Recommendations for the month ahead.

## Invoke

```
/aso-audit
```

You can also describe your goal naturally and let the router handle it:

```
/aso-router  Run an ASO audit for my app (id: 1617391485)
/aso-router  Why isn't my app ranking — can you review my listing?
/aso-router  Give me an ASO score for com.example.myapp
```

<Note>
  If you've already run `/app-marketing-context`, the audit will read your saved context file and skip redundant questions about your app. Run that skill first for the smoothest experience.
</Note>

## Inputs

The skill will ask you for the following information:

<ParamField path="app_id" type="string" required>
  Your app's identifier. For iOS, this is the numeric App ID (e.g. `1617391485`). For Android, this is the Google Play package name (e.g. `com.example.myapp`).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="target_country" type="string" default="US">
  The country market you want to audit against. Keyword rankings, competitor context, and chart positions are all country-specific.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="platform" type="string" default="iOS">
  Which platform to audit: `iOS`, `Android`, or `Both`. iOS and Android scoring differs — notably, the Keyword Field factor only applies to iOS, and Description carries more weight on Android (25% vs 5%).
</ParamField>

## Scoring Framework

The audit scores up to 10 factors and computes a weighted ASO Score from 0 to 100.

<Accordion title="10 Scoring Factors and Weights">

| Factor | iOS | Android | What's Evaluated |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Title** | 20% | 20% | Primary keyword presence, character usage (30-char limit), brand/keyword balance, readability |
| **Subtitle** | 10% | — | Secondary keywords not repeated from title, value proposition, character usage |
| **Keyword Field** | 10% | — | No repetition, no spaces after commas, singular forms, full 100-char usage |
| **Description** | 5% | 25% | First-3-line hook, feature/benefit framing, formatting, CTA; keyword usage on Android, where it is indexed |
| **Screenshots** | 15% | 15% | Slots used, first 3 lead with top features, readable captions, design consistency |
| **App Preview Video** | 5% | 5% | Exists, hooks in first 3 seconds, 15–30 seconds long, works without sound |
| **Ratings & Reviews** | 15% | 15% | Average rating, rating count against competitors, complaint themes in recent reviews |
| **Icon** | 5% | 5% | Distinctive, simple, fits the category, no text |
| **Keyword Rankings** | 10% | 10% | Top-10 keywords, near misses at ranks 11–50, 7-day trend, competitor gap |
| **Conversion Signals** | 5% | 5% | What's New, promotional text, In-App Events, Custom Product Pages |

A factor the audit has no data for is marked **Not assessed** and left out of the score. Public store data does not include the subtitle, keyword field, promotional text, preview video, In-App Events or Custom Product Pages; paste them in to have them scored. The overall score is the weighted average of the assessed factors and states how much of the weight it covers.

</Accordion>

## Output

The skill opens with a two-sentence verdict and the three most important actions, then returns these sections.

### ASO Score Card

A visual breakdown of your score across the factors the audit could assess, with one line of evidence under each:

```
Overall ASO Score: 74/100  (based on 6 of 10 factors, 70% of the weight)
Appeeky automated score: 98/100

Title:              8/10  ████████░░
Subtitle:           Not assessed (text not available)
Keyword Field:      Not assessed (text not available)
Description:        7/10  ███████░░░
Screenshots:        7/10  ███████░░░
Preview Video:      Not assessed
Ratings & Reviews:  8/10  ████████░░
Icon:               Not assessed (image not viewed)
Keyword Rankings:   7/10  ███████░░░
Conversion Signals: 5/10  █████░░░░░
```

### Quick Wins — Implement Today

Up to three changes you can make immediately without a new app submission — for example, updating your Promotional Text, Keyword Field, or responding to recent negative reviews.

### High-Impact Changes — This Week

Up to three higher-effort changes with significant ranking or conversion impact — such as rewriting your title and subtitle with better keyword targeting.

### Strategic Recommendations — This Month

Up to three longer-term improvements — such as building a screenshot set, launching an App Preview video, or running a rating-recovery campaign.

### Competitor Comparison Table

A side-by-side snapshot of your app versus the top 3 competitors on the metrics the audit pulled, such as rating and rating count.

### Not Assessed

What the audit could not see, and where to check it in App Store Connect or Play Console.

## MCP Tools Used

When the Appeeky MCP Server is connected, the audit pulls live data using these tools:

| Tool | What It Fetches |
|---|---|
| `aso_full_audit` | Full automated ASO audit scores and recommendations |
| `get_app` | App metadata — title, subtitle, description, screenshots, ratings |
| `get_app_keywords` | Current keyword rankings for the app |
| `get_keyword_metrics` | Search volume and difficulty for ranked keywords |

<Note>
  Without the Appeeky MCP Server connected, the skill works with metadata you paste in manually. Connecting Appeeky unlocks live keyword rankings, competitor data, and automated scoring.
</Note>

## Tips

<Tip>
  **Fix the Keyword Field first.** It's the most commonly under-optimized factor and requires no app submission — just an App Store Connect metadata update. A fully utilized, non-repetitive keyword field can move the needle on rankings within days.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Re-run the audit after each metadata update.** The skill tracks score changes over time, so you can measure the impact of every change you make. Aim to improve your score by at least 5–10 points per sprint.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Pair with `/keyword-research`** for any factor that scores below 6. The audit tells you *what* is wrong; keyword research tells you *which specific keywords* to use to fix it.
</Tip>
