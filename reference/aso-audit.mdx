The ASO Audit skill performs a comprehensive health check of your App Store or Google Play listing. It scores your app across 10 weighted factors on a 0–100 scale, identifies what's dragging your rankings down, and delivers a prioritized action plan — split into Quick Wins you can ship today, High-Impact Changes for this week, and Strategic Recommendations for the month ahead.

## Invoke

```
/aso-audit
```

You can also describe your goal naturally and let the router handle it:

```
/aso-skill  Run an ASO audit for my app (id: 1617391485)
/aso-skill  Why isn't my app ranking — can you review my listing?
/aso-skill  Give me an ASO score for com.example.myapp
```

<Note>
  If you've already run `/app-marketing-context`, the audit will read your saved context file and skip redundant questions about your app. Run that skill first for the smoothest experience.
</Note>

## Inputs

The skill will ask you for the following information:

<ParamField path="app_id" type="string" required>
  Your app's identifier. For iOS, this is the numeric App ID (e.g. `1617391485`). For Android, this is the Google Play package name (e.g. `com.example.myapp`).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="target_country" type="string" default="US">
  The country market you want to audit against. Keyword rankings, competitor context, and chart positions are all country-specific.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="platform" type="string" default="iOS">
  Which platform to audit: `iOS`, `Android`, or `Both`. iOS and Android scoring differs — notably, the Keyword Field factor only applies to iOS, and Description carries more weight on Android (15% vs 5%).
</ParamField>

## Scoring Framework

The audit scores 10 factors and computes a weighted ASO Score from 0 to 100.

<Accordion title="10 Scoring Factors and Weights">

| Factor | Weight | What's Evaluated |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | 20% | Primary keyword presence, character usage (30-char limit), brand/keyword balance, readability |
| **Subtitle** | 15% | Secondary keywords not repeated from title, value proposition, character usage — iOS only |
| **Keyword Field** | 15% | No repetition, no spaces after commas, singular forms, full 100-char usage — iOS only |
| **Description** | 5% iOS / 15% Android | First-3-line hook, feature/benefit framing, keyword density (Android), formatting, CTA |
| **Ratings & Reviews** | 10% | Overall rating, recency of reviews, sentiment trend, developer response rate |
| **Screenshots** | 10% | All 10 slots used, first 3 slots highlight top features, keyword-rich captions, design consistency |
| **App Preview Video** | 5% | Exists, hooks in first 3 seconds, 15–30 seconds long, works without sound |
| **Category** | 5% | Optimal primary and secondary category selection for maximum visibility |
| **Localization** | 5% | Metadata and keywords localized for target markets |
| **Ratings Momentum** | 10% | Trend direction of ratings over time — improving, stable, or declining |

</Accordion>

### Quick Wins — Implement Today

Three to five changes you can make immediately without a new app submission — for example, updating your Promotional Text, Keyword Field, or responding to recent negative reviews.

### High-Impact Changes — This Week

Three to five higher-effort changes with significant ranking or conversion impact — such as rewriting your title and subtitle with better keyword targeting.

### Strategic Recommendations — This Month

Three to five longer-term improvements — such as building a screenshot set, launching an App Preview video, or running a rating-recovery campaign.

### Competitor Comparison Table

A side-by-side snapshot of your app versus the top 3 competitors on key metrics including title keywords, screenshot count, rating, and estimated downloads.

| Tool | What It Fetches |
|---|---|

| `get_app` | App metadata — title, subtitle, description, screenshots, ratings |
| `get_app_keywords` | Current keyword rankings for the app |
| `get_keyword_metrics` | Search volume and difficulty for ranked keywords |

</Note>

## Tips

<Tip>
  **Fix the Keyword Field first.** It's the most commonly under-optimized factor and requires no app submission — just an App Store Connect metadata update. A fully utilized, non-repetitive keyword field can move the needle on rankings within days.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Re-run the audit after each metadata update.** The skill tracks score changes over time, so you can measure the impact of every change you make. Aim to improve your score by at least 5–10 points per sprint.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Pair with `/keyword-research`** for any factor that scores below 6. The audit tells you *what* is wrong; keyword research tells you *which specific keywords* to use to fix it.
</Tip>
