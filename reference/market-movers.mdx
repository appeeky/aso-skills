The Market Movers skill analyzes App Store and Google Play chart rank changes between snapshots to show you what's moving, how fast, and why. Whether you want to catch a breakout competitor before it dominates your category, time an update or campaign to a chart dip, or simply understand the rhythm of your category, this skill turns raw rank data into actionable intelligence.

## Invoke

```
/market-movers
```

You can also describe your goal naturally and let the router handle it:

```
/aso-skill  What apps are rising in the Health & Fitness charts right now?
/aso-skill  Show me the top gainers and losers in the Productivity category
/aso-skill  Which apps just entered the top 100 in the US?
```

<Note>
  If you've run `/app-marketing-context`, the skill knows your category and uses it as the default — you won't need to specify it every time.
</Note>

## Inputs

<ParamField path="chart_type" type="string" default="top-free">
  Which chart to analyze: `top-free`, `top-paid`, or `top-grossing`. Top-free reflects download volume; top-grossing reflects revenue.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="category" type="string" default="All">
  The App Store category to scope the analysis. Omit for an all-category overview, or specify (e.g. `Health & Fitness`, `Productivity`, `Games`) to focus on your competitive space.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="country" type="string" default="US">
  The country market to analyze. Chart dynamics vary significantly by country — a US gainer may not be moving at all in Germany.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="view" type="string" default="full overview">
  What you want back: `gainers` (only rising apps), `losers` (only declining apps), `new entries` (apps that just entered the top 100), or `full overview` (all movements).
</ParamField>

### Top Gainers

For each notable gainer, you get their rank change, current and previous position, and an explanation of what likely drove the surge — whether that's a viral moment, an Apple featuring, a UA campaign, a seasonal event, or a product update.

| App | Rank Change | Current | Previous | Category | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Calm | +45 | #3 | #48 | Health & Fitness | 4.8 |
| Streaks | +23 | #17 | #40 | Health & Fitness | 4.9 |

**Likely driver analysis** is included for each significant gainer — for example: *"Calm entered Apple's 'New Year, New You' featuring collection on January 1. The +45 position gain aligns with the feature start date. Expect the ranking to partially retrace when the collection rotates in ~2 weeks."*

### Top Losers

For each significant loser, the skill assesses what may have caused the decline and whether it represents an opportunity for you — an opening in the ranking you could target.

### New Chart Entries

| NovaSleep | #34 | Health & Fitness | 4.6 | 1,240 |

For each new entry, the skill assesses whether this is a fresh launch or a resurgent app, whether it competes directly with you, and what launch strategy they likely used.

## Tips

<Tip>
  **Run this weekly, not just when you're curious.** Chart dynamics compound. A competitor gaining 10 positions per week is doubling its visibility in a month. Catching a trend early gives you time to respond — whether by adjusting your UA spend, tightening your keyword targeting, or simply monitoring more closely.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Losers are opportunities.** When a competitor drops in the charts, they're ceding downloads to whoever fills the gap. If you can correlate the drop to a bad update or negative review surge, that's your window to capture their audience with a targeted UA push or updated keywords.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Check new entries for launch playbooks.** An app that enters the top 100 without being featured likely ran a coordinated launch — friends-and-family ratings, a Product Hunt launch, an influencer push, or a coordinated UA campaign. Reverse-engineer it by looking at their review dates and social activity.
</Tip>
