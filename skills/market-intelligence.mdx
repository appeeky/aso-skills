Market intelligence tells you what's happening in the App Store *right now* — which apps are climbing, which are falling, what keywords are spiking, and what Apple is featuring. Two skills power this view: **Market Movers** for focused chart-rank analysis, and **Market Pulse** for a complete market briefing that combines movers, keywords, featuring, and new launches in a single snapshot. Use them together to stay a step ahead of your competitors.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Market Movers">

    ## Track Chart Gainers, Losers & New Entries

    Market Movers zooms in on chart rank changes — who's rising fast, who's slipping, which apps just broke into the top 100, and which dropped out. For each significant movement it explains *why* the shift likely happened (viral moment, Apple featuring, ad campaign, seasonal spike) and what you can learn from it.

    **Invoke:** `/market-movers`

    ### What It Covers

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Top Gainers" icon="arrow-trend-up">
        Apps with the biggest positive rank movements, with analysis of what's driving the surge and whether the gain is likely to hold.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Top Losers" icon="arrow-trend-down">
        Apps shedding ranks fast — and whether each drop signals a competitive opportunity opening up for you.
      </Card>
      <Card title="New Entries" icon="sparkles">
        Apps that entered the top 100 for the first time. Understand their launch strategy and whether they compete in your category.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Dropped Out" icon="circle-minus">
        Apps that fell below the top 100 — useful for spotting gaps a well-timed push could fill.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    ### How to Use It

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Invoke the skill">
        Type `/market-movers` in your AI agent (Cursor, Claude Code, etc.).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set your scope">
        The skill asks for chart type (top-free, top-paid, or top-grossing), category, and country. Defaults to top-free / All Categories / US if you skip these.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose your view">
        Ask for a quick summary (3–5 bullet headline), a detailed report with full tables, or an alert-style digest you can share with your team.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Act on the insights">
        The skill surfaces an **Immediate Opportunity**, **Threat Assessment**, **Timing Insight**, and **Strategy Takeaway** tailored to your app based on what it finds in the data.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ### Example Prompt

    ```text
    What apps are rising in the free charts right now?
    Show me top gainers in Games and any new entries.
    ```

    ### Output Format

    The skill produces three formats on request:

    <Accordion title="Quick Summary (default)">
      Three to five bullet points covering the most important movements and their implications — perfect for a daily check-in.

      ```text
      🟢 GAINERS: Duolingo +45, Headspace +23, BeReal +18
      🔴 LOSERS: App D -32, App E -19
      🆕 NEW: App F entered at #7, App G at #34
      ⬇️ OUT: App H dropped from #89
      ```
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Detailed Report">
      Full tables for gainers, losers, new entries, and exits — with per-app analysis of likely drivers, sustainability of the move, and category-level volatility stats.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Recommendations Table">
      A prioritised action table (Priority → Action → Why → Expected Impact) you can paste directly into your sprint planning or share with your growth team.
    </Accordion>

    </Note>

  <Tab title="Market Pulse">

    ## Your Full App Store Market Briefing

    Market Pulse aggregates every major signal into a single view: chart movements, trending keywords, Apple featuring, new launches, and download benchmarks. Run it weekly to stay current on your category, or daily during a launch or competitive surge.

    **Invoke:** `/market-pulse`

    ### What It Covers

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Chart Dynamics" icon="chart-line">
        Top gainers and losers, new entries, and — if you have an app — your exact position, nearest rivals, and downloads needed to move up ten spots.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Trending Keywords" icon="magnifying-glass-chart">
        Keywords with rising search volume, tagged by growth rate, difficulty, and relevance to your category. Seasonal and event-driven trends are called out.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Apple Featuring" icon="star">
        App of the Day, Game of the Day, and active editorial collections — plus a read on whether Apple's current theme is a fit for your app.
      </Card>
      <Card title="New Launches" icon="rocket">
        Recent releases in your category with their current rank, rating, and days since launch — so you catch emerging threats early.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    ### How to Use It

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Invoke the skill">
        Type `/market-pulse` in your AI agent.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set your scope">
        Choose entire App Store or a specific category, your country (default: US), and your preferred format — quick briefing, detailed weekly report, or competitive focus.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review the headlines">
        The skill leads with 3–5 headline events: the most significant movement, any featuring impact, keyword shifts, and new threats or opportunities.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Read your category health check">
        Chart volatility, new entrant count (last 7 days), average top-10 rating, and download threshold give you a quick read on how competitive the moment is.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Run it weekly">
        Compare movers week-over-week, track which trending keywords sustained growth, and monitor whether featuring patterns forecast future trends.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ### Example Prompt

    ```text
    Give me a full market pulse for the Health & Fitness category —
    include chart movers, trending keywords, and featured apps.
    ```

    ### Output Formats

    <Accordion title="Quick Briefing (default)">
      A scannable digest with headlines, chart movers, trending keywords, today's featuring, and three actionable takeaways — one opportunity, one threat, one tactical next step.

      ```markdown
      ## App Store Pulse — [Date]

      ### 🔥 Headlines
      - New social app enters top 5 free
      - Apple featuring Health & Fitness apps this week
      - "AI photo editor" search volume surging +340%

      ### 📊 Chart Movers
      Top Gainers: Headspace +23, Calm +11
      Top Losers: App D -32
      New: App F entered at #7

      ### 📈 Trending
      Keywords rising: "meditation for sleep" (+180%), "habit tracker" (+95%)

      ### ⭐ Featured Today
      App of the Day: [App]
      Theme: Mindfulness & Wellness

      ### 💡 What This Means for You
      - Opportunity: competitor dropping — push keyword spend now
      - Watch: new entrant App F targeting your core keywords
      - Next step: submit for editorial featuring this week
      ```
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Detailed Weekly Report">
      All sections expanded with full data tables, competitor tracking across chart and keyword dimensions, and a strategic recommendation set for the week ahead.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Competitive Focus">
      The entire briefing filtered through your competitive landscape — how competitors moved in charts, which of their keywords are trending, whether any are being featured, and new competitive launches to watch.
    </Accordion>

    ### MCP Tools Used

    When the [Appeeky MCP Server](https://docs.appeeky.com/mcp) is connected, the skill pulls live data via:

    | Tool | What It Fetches |
    |------|----------------|
    | `get_market_movers` | Chart gainers, losers, new entries, and exits |
    | `get_market_activity` | All significant chart movements in chronological order |
    | `get_trending_keywords` | Keywords with rising search volume and growth rates |
    | `get_featured_apps` | What Apple is featuring today across all editorial slots |
    | `get_new_releases` | Recent app launches with rank, rating, and developer info |
    | `get_new_number_1` | Apps that just reached the #1 chart position |
    | `get_category_top` | Current chart standings for your category |
    | `get_downloads_to_top` | Download benchmarks needed to reach a target chart rank |

    <Tip>
      Run Market Pulse on the same day each week and compare snapshots. The delta — which keywords *stayed* trending, which movers *held* their gains — is often more valuable than any single snapshot.
    </Tip>
