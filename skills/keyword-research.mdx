The Keyword Research skill helps you move beyond guesswork and find the exact keywords that can drive downloads for your app. It expands from your seed keywords, scores every candidate on volume, difficulty, and relevance, calculates an Opportunity Score for each, and groups the results into primary, secondary, and long-tail tiers so you know precisely what to put in your title, subtitle, and keyword field.

## What you need before you start

- **App ID** — optional but recommended so the skill can compare your current rankings against opportunities
- **Target country** — defaults to US if not specified
- **Seed keywords** — 3–5 words or phrases that describe your app's core function
- **Intent** — are you optimizing for downloads, revenue, or brand awareness?

<Tip>
  The more specific your seed keywords, the better your results. Instead of "fitness", try "home workout", "strength training", or "workout tracker". Think about how your users describe the problem, not the solution.
</Tip>

## The research process

The skill works through four phases to build your keyword strategy:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Seed Expansion">
    Your seed keywords are the starting point, not the finish line. The skill expands them using Apple Search autocomplete suggestions (including variations like "[keyword] app", "[keyword] for [audience]", and "best [keyword]"), competitor keyword rankings, category-level analysis to surface terms top apps in your space are targeting, and synonyms — including the words users use to describe their *problem* rather than your *solution*.

    Long-tail suggestions from autocomplete often signal real user searches with lower competition than their head terms.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Keyword Evaluation">
    Every candidate keyword is evaluated across five signals:

    | Signal | What's checked | Why it matters |
    |--------|----------------|----------------|
    | **Search Volume** | Score 1–100 | More volume = more potential impressions |
    | **Difficulty** | Competition score 1–100 | Lower difficulty = faster path to ranking |
    | **Relevance** | How closely it matches your app | Irrelevant traffic doesn't convert |
    | **Intent** | Download intent vs informational | "photo editor app" beats "how to edit photos" |
    | **Current Rank** | Your existing position (if any) | Improving an existing rank is faster than starting from zero |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Opportunity Scoring">
    Each keyword receives an Opportunity Score that balances all three primary signals:

    ```text
    Opportunity = (Volume × 0.4) + ((100 - Difficulty) × 0.3) + (Relevance × 0.3)
    ```

    Volume and difficulty are on a 1–100 scale. Difficulty is inverted — a difficulty score of 20 contributes 80 points toward Opportunity. Relevance is a 1–100 assessment of how closely the keyword matches your app's actual function.

    The result is a single score that lets you rank every keyword from best opportunity to least, regardless of how the three underlying signals trade off.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tier Assignment">
    Keywords are grouped into strategic tiers so you know exactly where each one belongs in your metadata:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Primary Keywords" icon="trophy">
        Your top 3–5 keywords by Opportunity Score. These define your core positioning and must appear in your **title** or **subtitle**. They get the most algorithmic weight.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Secondary Keywords" icon="list-ol">
        5–10 keywords with strong opportunity scores. Target these in your **subtitle** and **keyword field**. These can rotate based on performance.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Long-tail Keywords" icon="tag">
        Remaining high-relevance terms — lower volume but very specific searcher intent. These fill out your **keyword field** and are often easier to rank for quickly.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Aspirational Keywords" icon="arrow-trend-up">
        High volume, high difficulty terms — the "dream" keywords. Track these over time as your app grows authority, but don't sacrifice primary keywords for them now.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the output looks like

The skill delivers a complete Keyword Research Report with three components:

**Top Keywords by Opportunity** — a ranked table showing every candidate with its Volume, Difficulty, Relevance, Opportunity Score, your current rank (if any), and the recommended tier:

```text
Keyword           Volume  Difficulty  Relevance  Opportunity  Rank  Action
meditation app      82        71          95         75.4       —    Primary
sleep meditation    74        58          90         73.4       34   Primary
mindfulness app     68        55          88         71.1       —    Primary
breathing exercises 61        44          82         68.8       —    Secondary
anxiety relief      77        79          75         63.7       —    Secondary
calm music          55        38          60         55.6       —    Long-tail
```

**Keyword Strategy** — a ready-to-use layout showing which keywords go in which metadata field, with character counts:

```text
Title (30 chars):      Calm: Sleep & Meditation App
Subtitle (30 chars):   Mindfulness, Anxiety & Stress
Keyword Field (100):   breathing,sleep,relaxation,focus,anxiety,stress,guided,yoga,wellness,nap
```

**Competitor Keyword Gap** — a table showing keywords your competitors rank for that you currently don't, so you can identify and close the most valuable gaps.

## Key rules the skill enforces

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="No keyword repetition" icon="ban">
    Don't repeat the same keyword across title, subtitle, and keyword field. Apple indexes each field separately — repetition wastes space without increasing your ranking.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Singular forms only" icon="one">
    Use singular forms in the keyword field. Apple automatically indexes both singular and plural, so "workout" covers "workouts" and saves you characters.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No spaces after commas" icon="compress">
    In the keyword field, write `meditation,sleep,calm` not `meditation, sleep, calm`. Each saved space is another character for another keyword.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Avoid wasted terms" icon="trash">
    Never include "app", "free", your app name, or App Store category names. Apple already knows these — they add zero ranking value.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Never include competitor brand names in your keyword field — it violates both Apple and Google's policies and can result in your app being removed from search results.
</Warning>
