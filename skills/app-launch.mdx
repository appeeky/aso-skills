Launching an app without a plan means leaving your best growth window to chance. The app-launch skill builds a complete, dated 8-week timeline around your specific launch date — covering ASO setup, TestFlight beta, press outreach, soft launch, launch day execution, and the critical first month of post-launch optimisation. Every phase comes with a prioritised checklist so you always know what to do next.

**Invoke:** `/app-launch`

## The 8-Week Launch Framework

<Steps>
  <Step title="Weeks 1–4: Pre-Launch Foundation">
    The work you do before launch determines your day-one ceiling. These four weeks cover everything from ASO to beta testing to building your press pipeline.

    **ASO Foundation (Week 1–2)**
    - Run keyword research to identify high-volume, winnable terms for your category
    - Write your title, subtitle, keyword field, and description with keyword intent
    - Run a competitor analysis to understand positioning gaps
    - Design all 10 screenshot slots (use `/screenshot-optimization`)
    - Create a 15–30 second app preview video
    - Design a distinctive, category-differentiated icon

    **Pre-Order Page (Week 1, new apps only)**
    - Set up your App Store pre-order page to begin collecting intent signals
    - Enable pre-order notifications so users get notified on launch day
    - Share the pre-order link across your channels for early sign-ups

    **Press & Community Prep (Week 2–3)**
    - Build a press kit: screenshots, icon, description, founder story, key stats
    - Identify 20–30 relevant journalists, bloggers, and YouTubers
    - Draft personalised pitch emails per outlet — not a single blast
    - Map 3–5 relevant communities (subreddits, Discord servers, forums) and begin contributing before you promote

    **Beta & Feedback (Week 3–4)**
    - Open TestFlight beta with 50–200 users
    - Collect feedback and fix every critical issue before public launch
    - Ask beta testers to be ready to leave reviews on launch day
    - Prepare your FAQ and support documentation

    **Content Pipeline (Week 3–4)**
    - Write your launch blog post
    - Prepare 10+ social media posts for launch week
    - Create a demo video optimised for social (vertical, hook in the first 2 seconds)
    - Draft your Product Hunt listing if applicable

    **Technical Setup (Week 3–4)**
    - Set up analytics to track downloads, retention, and in-app events from day one
    - Implement your in-app rating prompt — trigger it after a clear positive moment, never on cold open
    - Set up crash reporting so you can triage issues in real time on launch day
    - Test all in-app purchases and subscriptions end-to-end in a sandbox environment
  </Step>
  <Step title="Week 5: Launch Week">
    **Final Prep (T-7 days)**
    - Submit the app for review — allow a 2–3 day buffer for back-and-forth
    - Schedule your press embargo to lift on launch morning
    - Brief beta testers on the exact review-timing window
    - Set up App Store Connect for phased release (or full immediate rollout)
    - Prepare your Apple Search Ads campaign, ready to activate at launch

    **Launch Day — Morning**
    - Confirm the app is live (or phased release has started)
    - Send press emails as the embargo lifts
    - Post across all social channels — personal and app accounts
    - Submit to Product Hunt (if applicable — aim for 12:01am PST)
    - Post in each community you've been warming up
    - Email your list

    **Launch Day — Throughout the Day**
    - Monitor App Store Connect download numbers hourly
    - Respond to every review that comes in (use `/review-management`)
    - Engage every social media mention — reply, retweet, thank users
    - Watch crash reports in real time and triage anything critical
    - Share milestones publicly ("We hit #12 in Productivity!")

    **Launch Day — Evening**
    - Thank early adopters publicly
    - Share day-one stats if they're strong (social proof compounds)
    - Plan tomorrow's content and community posts
  </Step>
  <Step title="Weeks 6–8: Post-Launch Optimisation">
    **Week 6 (First week after launch)**
    - Continue daily social posting and community engagement
    - Respond to every review — the algorithm rewards responsiveness
    - Monitor keyword rankings: are you indexing for your targets?
    - Adjust Apple Search Ads bids based on first-week ROAS
    - Follow up with press contacts who didn't respond to the launch pitch
    - Submit to App Store editorial for featuring (`/app-store-featured`)

    **Weeks 7–8 (Month 1)**
    - Run a full ASO audit with real performance data (`/aso-audit`)
    - Adjust metadata based on actual keyword ranking data
    - A/B test screenshots with Product Page Optimisation (`/ab-test-store-listing`)
    - Plan your first major update based on user feedback themes
    - Evaluate paid acquisition ROI and scale or cut channels
    - Set up keyword tracking for ongoing rank monitoring
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Press Outreach

Getting press coverage on launch day creates a surge of high-quality installs and social proof that compounds for months. The skill generates personalised pitch templates for each outlet tier.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Pitch Email Structure">
    The skill produces a pitch email for each target outlet following this structure:

    ```text
    Subject: [Unique angle] — [App Name] launches [date]

    Hi [First name],

    [1 sentence on why this is relevant to their beat/audience]

    [App Name] is [one-line description of what it does differently].
    We're launching [date] — happy to give you early access today.

    Why it's a story:
    • [Angle 1 — data point, unique insight, or market timing]
    • [Angle 2 — founder story or underdog narrative]
    • [Angle 3 — user testimonial or beta stat]

    Press kit: [link]
    TestFlight: [link]

    Happy to jump on a 15-min call if useful.

    [Name]
    ```

    <Tip>
      Personalise the opening line for every recipient — reference their last relevant article by name. Generic pitches to "Dear Journalist" go straight to trash.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Outlet Tiers">
    The skill helps you categorise your press list by tier so you spend your personalisation energy where it matters most:

    **Tier 1 — High effort, highest impact**
    - TechCrunch, The Verge, Wired, Fast Company
    - Research the specific reporter covering your category
    - Offer exclusive embargo (one outlet, early access, full story)

    **Tier 2 — Category specialists**
    - MacStories, 9to5Mac, AppAdvice (Apple ecosystem)
    - Android Authority, Android Police (Android)
    - Relevant vertical press (health, finance, gaming publications)

    **Tier 3 — Community launchers**
    - Product Hunt — prepare your listing, hunter intro, and first-comment
    - Hacker News Show HN — strong for technical, developer, or innovative apps
    - Reddit — post in 3–5 relevant subreddits (contribute first, promote second)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Press Kit Checklist">
    The skill prompts you to assemble a press kit before outreach begins:

    ```text
    ✅ App icon — 1024×1024px PNG, no rounded corners
    ✅ Screenshots — all 10 slots, full resolution
    ✅ App preview video — 1080p MP4
    ✅ Founder photo — 400×400px minimum
    ✅ One-paragraph app description (150 words)
    ✅ One-sentence description (tweet-length)
    ✅ Key stats — beta users, 5-star reviews, notable metric
    ✅ Founder story — why you built this
    ✅ Download link — TestFlight for embargoed press
    ✅ Hosted at a public URL (Notion, Google Drive, or press page)
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Launch Amplification Channels

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Free Channels" icon="megaphone">
    **Product Hunt** — Best for productivity, developer, and design tools

    **Hacker News (Show HN)** — Technical or innovative apps

    **Reddit** — Warm up 3–5 subreddits weeks before you promote

    **Twitter/X** — Build-in-public launch thread

    **YouTube** — Demo video or "how I built this" story

    **App review sites** — AppAdvice, MacStories, 9to5Mac
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paid Channels" icon="dollar-sign">
    **Apple Search Ads** — Highest purchase intent; start here

    **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** — Best for consumer apps

    **TikTok** — Strong for younger demographics

    **Google UAC** — Broad reach across Android and Search

    **Micro-influencers** — 10K–100K followers often deliver the best ROI per dollar
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Success Metrics

The skill sets targets across three horizons to give you a clear picture of what success looks like:

| Horizon | Metrics to Track |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Week 1** | Total installs, App Store chart rank, keyword indexing, review count and rating |
| **Month 1** | Retention rate (D1/D7/D30), conversion rate, keyword rankings vs. targets, paid CAC |
| **Month 3** | Organic rank stability, LTV vs. CAC, subscription conversion (if applicable), category rank |
