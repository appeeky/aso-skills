Your screenshots are the single highest-leverage element on your App Store product page. Users spend just 3–6 seconds deciding whether to download — the first three screenshots (visible without scrolling) drive roughly 80% of that decision. The screenshot-optimization skill gives you a complete 10-slot strategy, ready-to-hand-off design briefs, and a competitor audit so you know exactly what you're up against.

**Invoke:** `/screenshot-optimization`

## Why Screenshots Now Rank, Not Just Convert

Apple's semantic search indexes the text visible in your screenshots. That means the words in your text overlays — your benefit headlines, feature callouts, and CTAs — are crawled and weighted in search ranking, not just seen by human eyes. A screenshot strategy built around keyword-rich, benefit-driven headlines does double duty: it converts browsers *and* improves organic visibility.

<Warning>
  Generic captions like "Push Notifications" or "Easy to Use" waste both a conversion opportunity and a keyword slot. Every headline should state a benefit and, where natural, include a target keyword.
</Warning>

## The 10-Slot Framework

The skill assigns a specific job to every screenshot slot. Here's the structure it applies:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Slot 1 — The Hook">
    The most important slot. Answer "What does this app do and why should I care?" in a single image. Effective patterns include a benefit headline paired with your key UI screen, a before/after transformation, or social proof combined with your main interface.

    **Avoid:** welcome screens, login screens, settings menus, or any screen that doesn't immediately communicate value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Slots 2–3 — Core Value">
    Show the two most compelling features with benefit-driven captions. These three slots (1–3) are visible without any scroll — treat them as a complete argument for the download.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Slots 4–7 — Feature Showcase">
    One feature per slot, each following the formula: `[Benefit Headline] + [Feature UI] + [Supporting Detail]`. Prioritise features your competitors don't highlight.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Slots 8–9 — Trust & Differentiation">
    Use these for social proof (awards, press mentions, user counts, ratings) and clear differentiation from alternatives — what you do that nobody else does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Slot 10 — Call to Action">
    Close with a CTA ("Start your free trial", "Join 5M+ users") or a recap of your top three benefits. Reinforce the reason to tap **Get**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Skill Produces

### Screenshot Plan

For each of the 10 slots, the skill outputs a structured brief you can hand directly to a designer or use to create assets yourself:

```text
Slot 1: The Hook
- Caption: "Sleep deeper — wake up refreshed"
- Screen: Sleep tracking dashboard (night view)
- Layout: Portrait, device centred, headline above
- Key element: Animated sleep score ring

Slot 2: Core Value #1
- Caption: "Know exactly when to sleep and wake"
- Screen: Smart alarm screen with optimal wake window
- Layout: Portrait, full-bleed UI, text overlay bottom-left
- Key element: Wake-time recommendation highlighted
```

### Design Brief

If you're briefing a designer, the skill produces a complete brief package including:

- **Color palette** drawn from your app's brand
- **Font recommendations** with minimum sizes for readability
- **Layout template** (portrait vs. landscape rationale for your app type)
- **Text overlay copy** for all 10 slots — character-counted and benefit-led
- **Device frame preference** and background style
- **Dark mode guidance** if your app supports it

## Design Best Practices

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Text Overlays">
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Do" icon="check" color="#16a34a">
        - Benefit-driven headlines (what the user gains)
        - 4–6 words per headline — short and scannable
        - Large, readable font (minimum 60px equivalent)
        - High contrast between text and background
        - Consistent font and style across all 10 slots
      </Card>
      <Card title="Don't" icon="xmark" color="#dc2626">
        - Feature names as headlines ("Push Notifications")
        - Long paragraphs or multi-sentence captions
        - Small text that's unreadable at thumbnail size
        - Text placed over busy or low-contrast backgrounds
        - Mixing fonts, weights, or sizes between slots
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Visual Design">
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Do" icon="check" color="#16a34a">
        - Clean, uncluttered UI — one focal point per screen
        - Consistent colour scheme tied to your brand
        - Modern device frames (or frameless for a premium feel)
        - Real app content — actual data, real use cases
        - Dark mode screenshots if your app supports it
      </Card>
      <Card title="Don't" icon="xmark" color="#dc2626">
        - Busy screens crammed with data or UI elements
        - Clashing colours or off-brand palettes
        - Outdated device frames (especially pre-iPhone 15 bezels)
        - Placeholder content, lorem ipsum, or empty states
        - Ignoring dark mode (a significant share of users browse at night)
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Layout Orientation">
    **Portrait (recommended for most apps)**

    Three proven layouts:
    - Device centred with benefit text above or below
    - Full-bleed UI with text overlay in a consistent corner
    - Split layout — text left, device right

    **Landscape (games, video, productivity tools)**

    - Full-screen gameplay or content moment
    - Minimal text overlay so the UI speaks for itself
    - Action-focused frames — show the most exciting moment

    <Tip>
      Landscape screenshots display larger on the App Store for the first three slots, making them especially effective for visually-driven apps like games.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Localisation">
    Each locale is a conversion experiment in itself:

    - Translate all text overlays — don't leave English copy in non-English markets
    - Adjust cultural references, imagery, and colour associations per region
    - Flip layouts for right-to-left languages (Arabic, Hebrew)
    - Use local currency if any screenshot references pricing
    - Consider localising device content (names, dates, maps) to look native
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## App Preview Video

<Accordion title="When to use an app preview video">
  App preview videos appear before your screenshots and autoplay silently. They work best for:

  - **Complex apps** that need a few seconds of motion to make sense
  - **Games** — almost always beneficial; show gameplay immediately
  - **Apps with unique interactions** (gestures, animations, live features)

  **Best practices:**
  - Hook in the first 3 seconds — lead with your most impressive feature
  - Keep it 15–30 seconds; longer previews lose viewers
  - Design for silent playback — add captions or text overlays, never rely on audio
  - Show real usage, not marketing animation
  - End with a clear CTA

  **When to skip it:**
  - Simple utility apps where screenshots tell the whole story
  - Apps where value is in content (news, podcasts, books) rather than UI
</Accordion>
