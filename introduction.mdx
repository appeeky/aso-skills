ASO Skills is an open-source collection of AI agent skills that turns your IDE's AI assistant into an expert App Store Optimization consultant. Instead of hunting through blog posts, hiring expensive consultants, or context-switching between tools, you install the skills once and ask your agent anything — from auditing your listing to researching keywords to planning a holiday push.

## The Problem It Solves

ASO knowledge is scattered. The best frameworks live in expensive courses, consultants' brains, and half-forgotten Medium posts. When you need to optimize a title, research keywords, or diagnose a ranking drop, you're either guessing or spending hours piecing together conflicting advice.

ASO Skills packages that expertise — scoring rubrics, output templates, and step-by-step frameworks — into structured skill files that your AI agent reads at the moment you ask a question. Connect the Appeeky MCP Server and the agent also pulls live App Store data: real keyword rankings, competitor metadata, download estimates, and trending terms. The result is actionable, specific guidance instead of generic advice.

## How It Works

Every skill is a `SKILL.md` file that contains a complete expert framework for one area of ASO. When you invoke a skill, your agent:

1. Reads the skill file to load the relevant framework, scoring rubric, and output template
2. Calls the Appeeky MCP Server to fetch live App Store data for your specific app and category
3. Applies the framework to your data
4. Returns structured, prioritized recommendations you can act on immediately

You can invoke skills by name — `/aso-audit`, `/keyword-research`, `/metadata-optimization` — or use the router skill (`/aso-skill`) to describe what you want in plain English and let it dispatch to the right specialist automatically.

## Skill Categories

ASO Skills covers the full lifecycle of an app — from pre-launch research to revenue optimization and market intelligence.

### ASO Core
The foundation of organic growth. The **aso-audit** skill scores your listing across 10 weighted factors and returns a prioritized fix list. **keyword-research** finds high-opportunity terms by volume, difficulty, and relevance. **metadata-optimization** writes your title, subtitle, and keyword field with three variants and exact character counts. **competitor-analysis** maps keyword gaps and creative opportunities. **seasonal-aso** builds a keyword calendar around trending moments. **android-aso** handles Google Play's unique ranking signals.

### Creative & International
Visual assets and global reach. **screenshot-optimization**, **app-preview-video**, and **app-icon-optimization** give you design briefs and A/B testing strategies for every slot. **localization** prioritizes markets and adapts your listing culturally. **custom-product-pages** unlocks Apple CPP strategy tied to your ad campaigns.

### Growth
From zero to launch and beyond. **app-launch** gives you an 8-week timeline with daily checklists. **apple-search-ads** and **ua-campaign** cover paid acquisition across all major channels. **creator-ugc-marketing**, **referral-program**, **web-to-app-funnel**, and **press-and-pr** round out the organic and earned growth playbook.

### Revenue & Retention
Turning installs into revenue and keeping users around. **monetization-strategy** benchmarks pricing tiers and paywall timing. **paywall-optimization** runs a 7-element conversion audit. **subscription-lifecycle** tackles churn, dunning, and win-back. **retention-optimization** and **onboarding-optimization** fix drop-off at every stage of the user journey.

### Analytics & Testing
Measuring what matters. **asc-metrics** connects to your exact App Store Connect data via Appeeky Connect. **app-analytics** builds your event tracking plan. **ab-test-store-listing** walks you through hypothesis-to-interpretation for store listing tests. **attribution-setup** handles SKAdNetwork 4 and MMP configuration.

### Market Intelligence
Knowing what's happening across the App Store. **market-movers** surfaces chart gainers, losers, and new entries. **market-pulse** delivers a full weekly briefing in one prompt. **competitor-tracking** runs ongoing surveillance on the apps you care about most.
